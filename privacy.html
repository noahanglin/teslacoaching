Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version
  • I only collect what I need to run your booking, and I never sell or share it for marketing.
  • Stripe handles payments, so I never see your card details.
  • The site's visitor stats are anonymous and don't use cookies.
  • Email me any time to see or delete your information.

What I collect when you book

  • Your email address and Discord username
  • Your name or preferred name, if you give one
  • Your rank and anything you write in the notes box
  • For VOD reviews: your replay code and in-game name
  • Your booking: the service, session time, your time zone and the price paid
  • From Stripe: confirmation that you paid, the amount and the email you paid with (never your card details)
  • If you use the Twitch subscriber discount: your Twitch username
  • If you leave a review: your rating, what you wrote, and the name you choose to show

How I use it

Only to run your booking: confirming it, sending your confirmation, reminder and follow-up emails, contacting you on Discord, delivering your review or improvement plan, handling reschedules and refunds, and keeping records of payments. Reviews only appear on the website if you tick the box that says I can feature yours, and then only with the name you chose. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Services that help run the site

  • Stripe processes payments. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach me. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Calendar): bookings are stored in my Google account, and emails are sent from it.
  • Twitch, only if you use the subscriber discount: when you log in, the site checks with Twitch whether you're subscribed to my channel. It can't post anything or see anything else, and your Twitch login isn't kept.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits anonymously, without cookies and without tracking you across sites.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Google Fonts provides its fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address when your browser loads the site.

Cookies and your browser's storage

The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. While you book, it saves your booking in progress in your browser's own storage, so your booking can still be confirmed if the page reloads after payment. Stripe's payment form may set cookies it needs to prevent fraud.

How long I keep it

I keep booking records for as long as I need them to run the coaching and keep proper records of payments. You can ask me to delete your information at any time. I'll delete everything except what I have to keep for payment and tax records.

Your choices

Email moiracoaching@gmail.com to get a copy of the information I have about you, correct it, or delete it.

Younger players

The site isn't meant for children under 13. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before booking (see the terms).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
