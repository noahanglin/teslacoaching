FAQ

Common questions

What do I need for a live session?

A Discord account with a working mic, and the game ready to play. We'll connect on Discord at your session time.

How do I get a replay code?
  1. From the main menu, open Career Profile.
  2. Go to the History tab, then Replays.
  3. Find the match you want reviewed. Click Share to generate a code.
  4. Choose Copy code, or write down the 6-character code shown next to it.
What games should I send in for review?

Any game you tried your hardest in and had a close outcome. I want to see your peak effort so I know how to push you even further. A close outcome tells me the matchmaking was likely more balanced.

Do you only coach Moira?

Moira is my specialty, but a lot of the lessons (positioning, game sense, cooldown tracking) carry over to other support heroes too. I can coach diamond rank and below for most heroes. My tank and dps roles peaked GM but I do not practice them often.

What if I need to reschedule?

Let me know at least 24 hours before your session and I'll move it to another open slot, no charge.
